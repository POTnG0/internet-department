Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026. This policy describes the personal Internet Department setup using OpenClip and the policy pages hosted on GitHub Pages.

When you connect TikTok, the integration requests basic account information to identify the connected account and upload permission to send the clip you select to its inbox. OpenClip stores TikTok credentials locally on the computer running the editor. It also keeps local copies of media, captions and upload status.

When you confirm an inbox upload, the selected video goes to TikTok. TikTok handles that copy under its own privacy policy. If you enable AI-assisted titles or moment selection, transcript text is sent to your configured AI provider. For the Gemini option, that provider is Google.

You control the local project files and can remove them when no longer needed. To stop future account access, revoke the integration in TikTok’s connected-app settings. Manage or delete copies already delivered to TikTok within TikTok. Avoid sharing the editor’s credential cache.

These static policy pages contain no sign-in form or analytics scripts. GitHub hosts the pages and may process visitor IP addresses and request information when serving them.

For questions, contact the operator through the project issues page. Issues are public. Keep account credentials and personal information private.
